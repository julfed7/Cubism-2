# Audio assets

Ten original, synthesized retro-style sound effects are included here as mono PCM WAV files. Gameplay loads them from `res://sounds/`. If a WAV is later removed, its event plays silently without interrupting the game.

| File | Duration | Type and sound direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shoot_pistol.wav` | 0.22 s | Pistol shot; dry 8-bit crack and short low thump. |
| `shoot_smg.wav` | 0.14 s | Compact automatic shot; sharp, clipped retro pop. |
| `shoot_shotgun.wav` | 0.38 s | Low noisy blast with a brief shell-like rattle. |
| `shoot_rifle.wav` | 0.26 s | Crisp high crack with a short echo. |
| `hit.wav` | 0.15 s | Bullet impact; hard tick with a low thud. |
| `zombie_hit.wav` | 0.24 s | Rough synthesized groan/grunt with a crunchy hit. |
| `zombie_death.wav` | 0.48 s | Descending 8-bit groan that fades quickly. |
| `pickup.wav` | 0.18 s | Bright two-note pickup chime. |
| `chest_open.wav` | 0.38 s | Short wooden creak and latch clicks. |
| `heal.wav` | 0.34 s | Soft rising 8-bit sparkle/chime. |
